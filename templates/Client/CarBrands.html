{# FAQ Item 1 #}

OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) marked tyres are bespoke models engineered in co-development between automotive manufacturers and premium tyre makers. Common examples include ★ for BMW, MO / MOE for Mercedes-Benz, N0 / N1 / N2 for Porsche, AO for Audi, and JLR for Jaguar Land Rover.

While non-OEM tyres of the identical dimensional size will fit safely, OEM tyres are specifically optimized for your vehicle's electronic stability control (ESC), all-wheel-drive torque transfer, and steering feedback. For high-performance sports cars and modern luxury vehicles, OEM tyres ensure factory ride quality and prevent premature or uneven tread wear.

{# FAQ Item 3 #}

You can find your factory tyre size in three easy ways:

  1. Driver Door Jamb Sticker: Open the driver's door and look for the manufacturer placard indicating standard front and rear tyre sizes, along with cold inflation PSI pressures.
  2. Tyre Sidewall: Check the markings embossed on your current tyres (e.g. 245/45 R19 102Y XL). Note that many sport and performance cars feature staggered sizes (wider tyres on the rear).
  3. TyresVision Car Brand Finder: Click on your vehicle manufacturer above to browse models, trims, and official factory wheel diameters.
{# FAQ Item 4 #}

Tyres sold in the UAE must pass rigorous GCC Standardization Organization (GSO) and ESMA standards. Non-GCC imported grey-market tyres often have rubber formulations suited for cold European climates, which can rapidly deteriorate and suffer blowouts on UAE summer asphalt reaching 60°C.

TyresVision supplies exclusively 100% genuine GCC-spec tyres with fresh manufacturing dates (less than 12 months from factory stamp) backed by full official warranty and vehicle registration testing compliance (RTA / Tasjeel pass guarantee).
