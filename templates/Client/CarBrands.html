Car Tyre Buying Guide

Finding the Right Tyres for Your Car in Dubai & UAE

Every vehicle brand and model comes with specific manufacturer specifications for tyre sizing, speed rating, and load capacity. Whether you drive a luxury Porsche 911, a family Toyota Land Cruiser, or an executive BMW 5-Series, TyresVision guarantees 100% genuine GCC-spec tyres tailored to your car.

What are OEM marked tyres and do I need them?

OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) tyres are custom-engineered in collaboration between tyre manufacturers and car brands. Examples include MO/MOE for Mercedes, ★ for BMW, N0/N1/N2 for Porsche, and AO for Audi. While standard tyres matching the correct size fit safely, OEM tyres optimize factory handling, braking, and ride comfort.

How does TyresVision mobile fitting work for my car?

Our mobile fitting vans come directly to your home, office, or designated parking in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Sharjah. Equipped with computerized tyre balancers, non-scratch rim clamps, and nitrogen inflation, our certified technicians change and balance your tyres on site with zero hassle.
